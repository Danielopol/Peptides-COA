# Pep Trust — 100 Blog Post Topics (SEO Content Plan)

**Goal:** get the Pep Trust site and app discovered on Google by ranking for the exact
questions people ask *before and while* deciding whether to trust a peptide COA — the
moment our tool is most useful.

**How this list is built (topic-cluster / hub-and-spoke model):**
Google rewards depth on a subject, not scattered one-offs. So the 100 topics are grouped
into **12 clusters**. Each cluster has one or two "pillar" posts (broad, high-volume) and
many "spoke" posts (specific, long-tail, lower competition). Every spoke should link up to
its pillar and out to 2–3 siblings, and every post should link to `/app/` with a
"Verify a COA — free" CTA. Internal linking is half the SEO value here.

**Publishing notes**
- Cadence: 2 posts/week is plenty. Front-load the **already-drafted** and **high-intent**
  clusters (1, 2, 7).
- Keep everything **RUO-compliant**: frame peptide-specific posts around *identity, molecular
  weight, purity, and COA/verification* — never dosing, benefits, protocols, or human use.
- Target 1,200–2,000 words for pillars, 700–1,200 for spokes. Add FAQ schema where natural.
- `✅ = already published` on the blog. Re-slot/rename freely.

Legend: **KW** = primary keyword to target · **Intent** = why they're searching.
